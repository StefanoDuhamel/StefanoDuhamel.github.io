Planning your trip

Traveling to Sicily

We’re so happy you’re making the journey.

01 / Fly

Arrive in Palermo

Palermo Airport (PMO) is the closest major airport to Scopello — allow approximately 45–60 minutes to reach the area by car. We recommend being in Sicily by August 31, ahead of the welcome party on September 1. Catania is on the other side of the island and requires a much longer drive, we do not recommend flying there.

02 / Get around

Plan your transfer

A rental car offers the most flexibility for exploring the coast. Taxis and private transfers are also available from Palermo Airport. Reserve your car or transfer in advance, and download an offline map before arriving—coastal roads can be winding and journeys may take longer than they appear on a map.

03 / A few essentials

Come prepared

A quick checklist for an easy arrival:

  • Check passport validity and entry requirements
  • Confirm accommodation and airport transfer
  • Bring a European power adapter
  • Review your phone plan or arrange an eSIM
  • Pack medications and essentials in your carry-on
  • Bring comfortable shoes, sunscreen and a light evening layer

First international trip?

We’ve got you.

Many of you are traveling a long way—and some may be leaving the United States for the first time. Open each guide below before booking so the journey feels simple and predictable.

When to arrive and how overnight flights work

Guests traveling from the United States, Canada or Mexico should arrive in Sicily no later than August 31. The celebrations begin September 1.

Flights from North America to Europe generally fly overnight and land on the following calendar day. If you leave Texas on August 30, you will normally arrive in Europe on August 31.

Allow extra time for delays, missed connections and jet lag. Do not plan to arrive on the day the celebrations begin. If you can arrive earlier and turn the wedding into a longer Italian vacation, even better.

Booking flights from Texas

Your final destination

Search from your home airport to Palermo Falcone Borsellino Airport (PMO). There are no direct flights from Texas to Palermo, so expect at least one connection. Book the complete itinerary together—you do not need to purchase each flight separately.

Your connection may be through Rome, Milan, Paris, Frankfurt, Munich, Madrid, London or another European hub. Avoid very short international connections: allow time for immigration, security, terminal changes and unfamiliar airports.

Do I need to stay overnight during my connection?

No. Most guests can connect in Europe and continue directly to Palermo. A night or two in the connecting city is optional if you would enjoy breaking up the journey.

What about Trapani?

Trapani–Birgi Airport (TPS) is also about 52 km from the venue and may suit some itineraries, but Palermo generally offers more practical options from North America. We do not recommend Catania; it is on the other side of Sicily.

From Palermo Airport to Scopello

After landing, allow approximately 45–60 minutes by car to reach Scopello. We recommend either:

  • Renting a car if you want independence and plan to explore Sicily
  • Reserving a private transfer if you are not comfortable driving in Italy

Please do not rely on Uber around Scopello. Book your car or transfer well in advance because September remains a busy travel period.

Renting a car : important tips for American guests
  • Reserve an automatic transmission unless you are comfortable driving manual; automatics are less common and should be booked early
  • Choose a small or medium-size car; Sicilian streets and parking spaces are much narrower than those in Texas
  • Check Italian driver’s-license and international driving-permit requirements well before traveling
  • Download Google Maps and the Sicily area for offline use before leaving the airport
  • Never leave luggage or valuables visible in a parked car
Driving in Sicily

Highways are generally straightforward, but roads become narrower inside towns and villages. Drive slowly and expect a different pace from home.

Historic centers may contain ZTLs—Zona a Traffico Limitato. These restricted zones are monitored by cameras. Do not enter unless you know your vehicle is authorized.

Where to stay and what to wear

Stay in or near Scopello or Castellammare del Golfo so you remain close to the festivities and planned transportation areas. Review our Where to stay recommendations before booking.

For wedding clothing, see the Dress code section. Tonnara di Scopello is a historic seaside property with natural stone and uneven ground, so keep the terrain in mind when choosing shoes.

Passports and entry requirements

U.S., Canadian and Mexican guests need a valid passport to travel to Italy. If you do not have one, apply well before 2027 rather than waiting until the wedding approaches.

Entry rules can change. Check the official Italian and European requirements for your nationality before traveling; we will update the website closer to the wedding if a European travel authorization is required.

Keep your passport with you while traveling—never put it in checked luggage.

Money and paying in Italy

Italy uses the euro (€). Cards are widely accepted, but carry a small amount of cash for cafés, parking and small purchases.

  • Tell your bank you are traveling if required
  • Check foreign-transaction fees and know your card PIN
  • Bring at least two payment methods and keep them separately
  • If a terminal or ATM offers dollars or euros, choose euros
Phones, data and useful apps

Check whether your phone plan includes international data. Options include your carrier’s international plan, an international eSIM, or a European SIM/eSIM.

  • Google Maps for navigation—download Sicily for offline use
  • Google Translate for translations
  • WhatsApp, commonly used in Italy
  • Your airline’s app for boarding passes and flight updates

Make sure you will have mobile data when you land.

Electricity and adapters

Italy uses Type C, F and L outlets and 230V electricity. American plugs require a European/Italian adapter.

Most modern phone and laptop chargers accept both voltages, but check the device label. An adapter only changes the plug shape—it does not convert voltage. American hair dryers and curling irons may not be compatible, so check carefully or leave them at home.

Packing checklist for Sicily
  • Passport, wallet, cards and required driving documents
  • Travel-insurance, flight and accommodation confirmations
  • Prescription medications in their original labeled packaging
  • European adapters, phone charger and portable battery
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen, swimsuit and comfortable walking shoes
  • Light layers for warm Mediterranean days and cooler evenings
  • Wedding attire and wedding shoes

Scopello has natural stone, cobblestones and uneven surfaces, so keep the terrain in mind when choosing shoes.

Carry-on luggage and medications

Pack your wedding outfit, essential shoes, medications and valuables in your carry-on. Checked bags can be delayed, particularly on connecting international itineraries.

Bring enough prescription medication for the whole trip plus a little extra for delays. Keep it in your carry-on and, when possible, in its original labeled packaging.

Eight golden rules for a first international trip
  • Check or apply for your passport now
  • Leave plenty of time for international connections
  • Arrive in Sicily no later than August 31
  • Keep your passport with you, never in checked luggage
  • Keep your wedding outfit in your carry-on
  • Download boarding passes and important reservations
  • Have mobile data available when you land
  • Expect things to work a little differently—that is part of the fun
A few helpful Italian words
  • Ciao — Hello / Goodbye
  • Buongiorno — Good morning / Good day
  • Buonasera — Good evening
  • Grazie — Thank you
  • Per favore — Please
  • Scusi — Excuse me
  • Parla inglese? — Do you speak English?
  • Dov’è…? — Where is…?
  • Il conto, per favore — The check, please
  • Aiuto! — Help!

You do not need to speak Italian, but a warm buongiorno and grazie go a long way.

Give yourself time, enjoy the slower pace, and come ready for the sea, good food and a beautiful celebration.

Tonnara di Scopello

Our Venue

The village of Scopello took the form we know today in the seventeenth century, but this coastline carries a much older story. The Tonnara began as a small tuna-fishing complex around the thirteenth century and grew through the hands of the Sanclemente family, the Jesuits, and later the Florios. Its stone baglio, fishermen’s quarters, boats, and watchtower preserve centuries of maritime life beneath the Faraglioni.

While you’re here

Things to do

A few ways to turn the wedding into a Sicilian escape.

  • Hike the Zingaro Reserve ↗Enter from the Scopello side for a spectacular coastal trail and turquoise swimming coves. Bring sturdy shoes, water, sunscreen, and a swimsuit.
  • Swim at the Tonnara ↗Explore the open-air museum, original fishing boats, and historic rooms before swimming beneath the Faraglioni. Daily access is limited, so reserve ahead.
  • Wander Castellammare del GolfoStroll around the harbor and Arab-Norman castle, stop for granita or seafood, and spend a relaxed afternoon at Cala Petrolo, Cala Marina, or Plaja beach.
  • See the coast from the waterBook a half-day boat trip from Castellammare to the Faraglioni, Zingaro’s hidden coves, and San Vito Lo Capo—with plenty of time for swimming.
  • Visit ancient SegestaAbout a 30-minute drive inland, Segesta’s remarkably preserved Greek temple and hillside theater make an easy cultural half-day trip.
  • Take a day trip to Erice ↗Explore medieval lanes, churches, the Castle of Venus, and sweeping views above Trapani—then try a warm genovese pastry before heading back.

Are kids welcome?

How do I get to Scopello?

Fly into Palermo Falcone Borsellino Airport (PMO), then rent a car for the easiest and most flexible journey to Scopello. Alternatively, Autoservizi Russo operates coach connections from Palermo Airport to Castellammare del Golfo and also serves Scopello; airport buses depart from the clearly marked stops outside Arrivals, to the right, and tickets for this route are purchased onboard. Service may be seasonal, so please check the latest timetable before traveling and plan a taxi or transfer for the final connection if needed. We recommend avoiding Catania Airport, which is on the opposite side of Sicily and requires a much longer drive.
